# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, sexualization of minors, and hostile or degrading content
are not welcome.

When discussing visual quality or character interpretation, critique the
artifact and the documented design goal rather than the contributor. Respect
copyright, attribution, privacy, and takedown requests.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict
participation when necessary to protect contributors and the project.
